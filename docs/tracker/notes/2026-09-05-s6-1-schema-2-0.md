# S6.1 Schema 2.0 Bump

**Date:** 2026-09-05

## What We Did
- Bumped `schema_version` to "2.0".
- Added `household` containing `tenure` and `existing_equipment`.
- Added `safety_points` to `Floor` (for utility shutoffs, etc.) with geometric positioning and unique IDs.
- Added `secured` boolean to `FloorObject`.
- Added `last_scanned` to `Meta`.
- Extended `ProfileTag` with `hearing_impaired` and `vision_impaired`.
- Maintained exact lockstep across `schema/homemodel.schema.json`, `backend/app/models.py`, and `escape_plan/Models/HomeModel.swift`.
- Added decoding upgrade logic in Pydantic (`HomeModel.model_validate`) and Swift (`init(from decoder:)`) to treat 1.1 JSON documents as valid 2.0 models with missing optional fields initialized to defaults.
- Updated all JSON fixtures (`bungalow`, `apartment`, `twostory`, `jagged`) to `schema_version` "2.0". Seeded `twostory.json` with rent tenure and safety points, and added a secured wardrobe.
- Verified backend Pydantic tests, EditorCore Swift tests, Corpus validation, and iOS app tests.
- Updated `docs/profile-constraint-mappings.md` to clarify that sensory tags remain routing-neutral.

## Why We Did It
To support the "readiness pivot" (S6 Phase 3). The AI must know household facts, the locations of safety points like gas and water shutoffs, and sensory conditions of the residents to compute accurate preparedness checklists (in S6.4) and peril analysis (in S6.3).
