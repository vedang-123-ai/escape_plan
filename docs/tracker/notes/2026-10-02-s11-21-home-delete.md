**What sync actually does on a delete (nuance 6, read before writing any wording).** `HomeLibrary.delete` tells
`HomeDocumentSyncRepository.homeWasDeletedLocally`, which writes a tombstone carrying the home's owner from
`SyncState.homeOwners`. `SyncManager` sends `DELETE` only when that owner is the signed-in account. So "signed out =
this iPhone only" is not quite the rule: a home owned by an account that is signed out right now is deleted from that
account the next time it signs in here. Three cases, one pure function: `Sync/HomeDeletionReach.swift` (EditorCore).

**Placement and wording (Anish, live in the simulator).** Two options shown before building: (A) a context menu on the
