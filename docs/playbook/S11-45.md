switch to B, send one message, and A's thread is overwritten and lost. Every send posts the full history, so long
threads send ever more tokens per turn. After S11.26 they hit the server's cap and get a 422. Before it they hit the
model's context limit and got a 502 "AI rejected". Either way the thread is stuck until "New chat". Saving the
conversation uses `try?`, so a failed save is silent (AUD-049).
