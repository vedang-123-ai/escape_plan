# S11.1: Earthquake + tornado corpus depth, and cited prevention tips · ~6h · Lane A · Prereqs: baseline (blocks S11.2, S11.5 tips)

Context: the corpus is 6 fire docs against 1 earthquake and 1 tornado, and `egress_thresholds.json` has no
codified numbers for the last two. The product now promises fire as *code-quantified* and earthquake/tornado
as *FEMA/NOAA-grounded readiness*, so the second tier must be as deep and as honest as the sources allow.
This session also authors the prevention tips a firefighter asked for (meeting spot, close doors behind you,
call 911, do not fight a large fire, do not grab valuables).
