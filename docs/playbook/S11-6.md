# S11.6: Responder Snapshot v2, "Share with your fire department" · ~7h · Lane B · Prereqs: baseline

Context: the firefighter's best idea was an opt-in path where a family shares its home information with its
own department. He also said stored layouts go stale and that departments cannot act on self-reported data.
The existing Responder Snapshot (`escape_plan/Export/ResponderSnapshot*`, `ConsentSheet`) already exports floor
plan, utility shutoffs and vulnerable-occupant counts by room offline. This session makes it the headline
feature and makes its honesty impossible to miss.
