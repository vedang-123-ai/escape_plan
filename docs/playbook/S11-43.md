# S11.43: Signing in lasts, and when a session ends the user is told · ~3h · Lanes D + E · Prereqs: S11.16 (copy), S11.28 (api_auth.py serial)

Context: access tokens live 60 minutes (`JWT_ACCESS_TTL_MINUTES` in render.yaml) and there is no refresh. One hour
after signing in, the next launch or sync quietly discards the session; the only signal is the badge changing to
"Not syncing". The gate is correctly not shown again (S8 ruling), but nothing else says so, while the gate's copy,
"Back up your homes?", implies an ongoing backup. Users believe they are backed up when they are not.
