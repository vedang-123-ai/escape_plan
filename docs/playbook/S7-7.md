S7.7 — Seeing the hazard: 3D emphasis and the device pass · ~6h · Prereqs: S7.4, S7.5, S7.6

Context: a checklist row saying "the bookcase can reach your bed" lands ten times harder when
tapping it swings the camera to THAT bookcase in the dollhouse. This session closes the loop
from finding to object in the 3D view, and then does what no simulator can: verifies the whole
object story on hardware, extending the standing LiDAR field-trip procedure to objects. The
constraint that shapes every visual choice: the White Model overhaul exists because colour
proliferation was destroying the scene. Route orange is reserved for lit routes and exits;
advisory chrome uses the dark amber Theme already defines; there are NO new colours.
