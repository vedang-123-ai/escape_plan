# S11.22: Sync makes at most one conflict copy, and never while the home is open · ~3h · Lane D (iOS) · Prereqs: S11.19, S11.21 (blocks S11.32)

Context: when a sync conflict resolves in the remote's favour, `SyncManager` first duplicates the local home as
"<name> (this device)" and then tries to write the pulled copy. If the home is open in House, the write is refused,
the sync record is not updated, and the next sync plans the same pull and makes another copy. With the conflicting
home open, every foreground or launch sync adds one more copy; each uploads on the next sync and downloads to every
other device. Until S11.21 there was no way to delete them.
