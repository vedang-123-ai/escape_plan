# Home fire statistics (grounded compilation)

Every statistic below carries its source. These numbers ground the AI's "why this matters" explanations and are the approved facts for the demo video / pitch (S5.1). **Do not use statistics not listed here without adding them to the corpus with a source.**

## Deaths and smoke alarms

- Almost **3 out of 5 home fire deaths** occur in fires in homes with **no smoke alarms (41%) or no working smoke alarms (16%)**. [cite: nfpa-smoke-alarms-report]
- The death rate per 1,000 reported home structure fires is **~55% lower** in homes with working smoke alarms than in homes with none or failed alarms. [cite: nfpa-smoke-alarms-report]

## Time to escape

- You may have **as little as 2 minutes** to escape a home fire from the moment the smoke alarm sounds. [cite: redcross-two-minutes]
- Home fires are the **most frequent disaster** in the United States. [cite: redcross-two-minutes]

## Planning gap (the problem the app addresses)

- Only about **26% of families** have developed **and practiced** a home fire escape plan. [cite: redcross-escape-fact-sheet]
